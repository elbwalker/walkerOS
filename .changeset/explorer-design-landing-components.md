`@walkeros/explorer/design/components` gains the building blocks of the walkerOS
home page: `Icon`, `InstallCommand`, `Card`, `Eyebrow`, `Stat`, `Text`,
`TextLink`, `CheckList`, `SectionHeading`, `ProblemCard`, `FeatureItem`,
`FaqItem`, `PlanCard`, `CaseCard`, `HighlightCard`, `Section`, `CardGrid`,
`Split`, `Cluster`, `Hero`, and the three home page demos (`HeroTaggingViz`,
`ArticleTeaserTracking`, `DestinationMappingViz`). Every design component
forwards tagging attributes to its root.
