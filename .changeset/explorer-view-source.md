New `ViewSource` component: wrap a tagged element, hover it and toggle to its
live HTML with the `data-elb` attributes, edited in place. Back in Visual the
element renders your edit and walkerOS picks up the new tags; a Reset icon
restores the original. New `ToggleButton`: one button that names the option a
click switches to, at a fixed width.
