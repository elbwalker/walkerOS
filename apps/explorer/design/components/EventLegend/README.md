The fixed colour key for the five parts of a walkerOS event, as used in every product visualisation.

Geist Mono 11px in `viz-fg-2` on `viz-bg`; each part pairs its `event-*` swatch with its name. Stays dark in both themes.

**Consumer provides:** optional `parts` to show a subset ('entity' | 'action' | 'property' | 'context' | 'globals').

Never reuse these hues for status or decoration.
